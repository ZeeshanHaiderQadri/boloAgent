---
title: "Email senders"
description: "Connect the mailbox Bolo sends campaign email from"
---

An email sender is the mailbox Bolo uses to send from. You can add more than
one sender; one is marked as the default for new campaigns.

Open **Email → Senders** in the app, choose **Add sender**, and pick a provider.

## SMTP

SMTP works with Hostinger and any standard mail host. The sender dialog includes
a Hostinger preset that fills in `smtp.hostinger.com`, port `465` and SSL.

You need:

- the SMTP host and port from your mail provider;
- the mailbox username and password (or an app password, if your provider
  requires one);
- a From address on that mailbox, and optionally a Reply-to address.

Use **Verify** to test the connection and **Send test** to send a test message.
Test messages should go to a mailbox you control.

<Note>
  Plain SMTP does not report bounces or spam complaints back to Bolo. Monitor
  the sending mailbox, remove bad addresses, and keep the list clean manually.
  SES can report these events through AWS.
</Note>

### Use a separate domain for cold outreach

Send cold outreach from a separate domain or mailbox, not your main business
domain. Spam complaints and reputation damage follow the domain and IP that
sent the mail. A dedicated outreach domain keeps a bad campaign from affecting
the address your customers already know.

## Amazon SES

The SES adapter is implemented, but it has **not been tested against a live AWS
account** in this build. Treat it as untested: use SMTP for production sending
until an SES account has been connected and verified end to end.

An SES sender needs an AWS access key, a region, and a verified From address or
domain. A new SES account starts in the sandbox and can only send to verified
addresses until AWS grants production access.

## Limits

Each sender has a conservative daily cap and per-second rate. You can lower
them for a new mailbox; raising them beyond the provider's own limits will
cause sends to fail. The campaign engine counts deliveries per sender and
resets the daily count at midnight UTC.
